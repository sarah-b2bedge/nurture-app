Nurture Assistant

Baby care tracking that feels like talking.

A mobile-first UX flow for new parents who need quick, safe logging with voice-style input, secure account management, subscription checkout, editable AI confirmations, and medicine safeguards.

Fast One primary assistant input plus quick add buttons for repeat actions.
Safe Medicine logs require confirmation of child, medicine, dose, and time.
Simple Rounded cards, short copy, and defaults that reduce typing.
Supportive Warm colors and reassuring states built for tired caregivers.
Secure Account, password, subscription, and payment flows use clear trust cues.
